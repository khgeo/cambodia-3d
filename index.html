Live flights: how it works
Live positions come from the open ADS-B exchanges (adsb.lol, adsb.fi) and are refreshed every 10 s. Most of these sites do not allow web pages to read them directly, so the page may need a small proxy. Make a free Cloudflare Worker with the code in tools/flight-proxy.js and paste its address here (ending in ?url=).
